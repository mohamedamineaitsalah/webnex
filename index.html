04 Our process

From a spark of an idea
to a live website.

  1. We listen first. Your goals, audience, content and timeline become a clear plan and sitemap — so everyone knows exactly what we're building and why.

  2. Wireframes shape the structure, then visual design brings it to life — colours, typography and layouts that fit your brand. You review and refine with us.

  3. We turn approved designs into fast, responsive, maintainable code — with smooth interactions and a structure that's easy to grow.

  4. Every page is checked across devices and browsers. We optimise images, speed and accessibility, and make sure every link and form works.

  5. We put your website live and stay by your side afterwards for updates, improvements and questions.
